The mini course

Four short modules that take you from a phone in your hand to a printed heirloom portrait. Watch them in an afternoon*, then make your first portrait the same day.

  1. 1

    Camera settings*

    The few settings to change on your phone so the photo comes out sharp and true to color. About 12 minutes*.

  2. 2

    Capturing magic*

    Where to stand your child, which window to use, and how to get a real smile. About 18 minutes*.

  3. 3

    Easy editing in the app*

    Every step of the app, in order: cutout, outfit, pose, eyes, hand tint, vignette and save. About 25 minutes*.

  4. 4

    Printing and gifting your heirloom*

    Upscale to 4K*, choose a size and a paper, and frame it the classic way. About 10 minutes*.

What you need

  • A phone. No fancy equipment required.
  • A window and a plain wall.
  • A white outfit if you have one. If not, the app has heirloom outfits for girls and for boys.
