Privacy policy

Draft*. Every line on this page needs Mel’s approval and a check against how the app really works before the app is submitted.

Who we are

Childhood Heirloom Portraiture is run by Lizzie Mac Studio*. You can reach us at hello@childhoodheirloomportraiture.com*.

Your photos

Most editing happens on your phone, and those photos never leave it*. A few steps use AI, such as changing the outfit. For those, the photo is sent to our AI provider for that one edit and is not kept or used to train anything*.

What we collect

Your course code and your credit balance, so the app knows what you have*. We do not ask for your name, your contacts or your location*.

Children

The app is for parents and grandparents, not for children to use. We do not knowingly collect information from children*.

Purchases

Credits are bought through the App Store or Google Play*. We never see your card details.

Your choices

Write to us and we will delete anything we hold about you within 30 days*.

Changes

This policy took effect on November 27, 2026*. If it changes, the new date will show here.
