As moms, we know first-hand the busy day-to-day pace in raising children. It’s so easy to let time slip through our fingers. Before we know it, our children are grown and we wonder where the time went!

Good to know

Will it arrive before Christmas?

Yes, if you order by Cyber Monday and finish your portrait by December 10*. Your card is printed and sent to your US address* to arrive before Christmas*. If it is late, we refund you*.

Do I need a real camera?

No. Your phone is all you need. The course shows you the few settings to change.

What happens to my child’s photo?

Your photo stays on your phone. When you use an AI step, the photo is sent for that one edit and is not kept afterward.*

Which phones does it work on?

The app is made for iPhone first*. An Android version follows*.

What if I don’t love it?

Write to us within 14 days and we will refund you.*

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Buy the bundle and forward your welcome email to her*. See the Christmas page for ideas.

How does the Christmas card work?

Make your portrait in the app and send it to us by December 10*. We print it as a 5 x 7 card on heavy stock with an envelope* and mail it to your US address*. Send it on to Grandma, or keep it as a memory.

Does it work for boys?

Yes. There are heirloom outfits for boys and for girls.

What is an AI credit?

Some steps, like changing the outfit, use AI. Each of those uses one credit. Everything else you do on your phone is unlimited.

Can I print my portrait?

Yes. Upscale it to 4K in the app first*, then print it wherever you like.
