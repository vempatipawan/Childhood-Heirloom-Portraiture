A portrait your family keeps

As moms, we know first-hand the busy day-to-day pace in raising children. It’s so easy to let time slip through our fingers. Before we know it, our children are grown and we wonder where the time went!

White vignetted heirloom portraits are a tradition made famous in the Southern states: a child, a white outfit, a soft white fade, and nothing else. Now you can make one yourself, at home, on your own schedule.

Good to know

Will my cards come in time?

Yes, if your finished portrait reaches us by Friday, December 4*. Your cards are printed and sent to your US address* to arrive by Monday, December 14*. If anything will be late we tell you first, and you choose a refund or New Year cards*.

I’ve never made a portrait. What if mine isn’t good enough for a card?

That is what the course is for, and it is short. If you are one of the first 50, Mel looks at yours herself before it prints*. And if you don’t love it, you get your money back before anything is printed*.

What if my little one won’t sit still?

Most don’t. The Wiggly Child Cheat Sheet is one page for exactly that*.

Do I need a real camera?

No. Your phone is all you need. The course shows you the few settings to change.

What happens to my child’s photo?

Your photo stays on your phone. When you use an AI step, the photo is sent for that one edit and is not kept afterward*. The finished portrait you send us for your cards goes to us and to the printer, and nowhere else*. We delete our copy 14 days after your cards arrive*.

Which phones does it work on?

The app opens in the browser on your phone, iPhone or Android*. There is nothing to install*.

What if I don’t love it?

Write to us within 14 days and we refund every dollar, before anything is printed*.

Can I order more than 25?

Yes. Add 25 more at checkout, or choose the Grandmother Collection for 50*.

I have two grandmas to think of.

Every paid bundle comes with Grandma’s 8 x 10, ready to print, so you can print one for each*. Or add a framed one and we have it delivered*.

Does it work for boys?

Yes. There are heirloom outfits for boys and for girls.

What is an AI credit?

Some steps, like changing the outfit, use AI. Each of those uses one credit. Everything else you do on your phone is unlimited.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Buy the Heirloom Kit and forward your welcome email to her*. See the Christmas page for ideas.

Can I print my portrait myself?

Yes. Every paid bundle comes with a print-ready file*, so you can print it wherever you like.

Still wondering? Write to Mel at hello@childhoodheirloomportraiture.com*
