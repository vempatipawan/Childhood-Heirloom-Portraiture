Good to know

Will my cards come in time?

Yes, if your finished portrait reaches us by Friday, December 4*. Your cards arrive by Monday, December 14*. If anything will be late we tell you first, and you choose a refund or New Year cards*.

Do I have to design the card?

No. You pick one of our twelve designs, send us your portrait and your child’s name, and we make the card. We send you a picture of it, and nothing prints until you say yes.

I’ve never made a portrait. What if mine isn’t good enough?

That is what the course is for, and it is short. If you are one of the first 50, Mel looks at yours herself before it prints*. And if you don’t love it, you get your money back before anything is printed*.

What happens to my child’s photo?

When you add a portrait on our card pages, it stays in your own browser. The finished portrait you send us for your cards goes to us and to the printer, and nowhere else*. We delete our copy 14 days after your cards arrive*.

Does it work for boys?

Yes. There are heirloom outfits for boys and for girls, and every design works for both.
