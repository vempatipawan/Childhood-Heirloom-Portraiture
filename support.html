How do I send my portrait for my cards?

Save your finished portrait in the app, then reply to your welcome email with it, your child’s name and your mailing address, by Friday, December 4*.

Can I change my card after I approve it?

Once you reply yes, your cards go to print, so look closely at the picture we send you first*. Before that, change anything you like.

My cards have not arrived.

Write to us with your name and we will look up your tracking the same day*. If they reach you after December 18, your money comes back and the cards are yours*.

How do I bring my own photo into the app?

On the first screen, tap Select a photo* and choose one from your phone’s photos. Then tap the child you want to keep.*

What is an AI credit?

Some steps, like changing the outfit, use AI. Each of those uses one credit. Everything else you do on your phone is unlimited.

How do I use my course code?

Open the app, tap the credits star at the top right, then Enter course code*. Type the code from your welcome email* and your credits appear.

How do I get more credits?

Tap the credits star at the top right and choose a pack: 50 credits for $9.99* or 100 credits for $17.99*.

My portrait looks soft or small when printed.

Use the print-ready file from your welcome email*, or write to us and we will send it again.
